Personal rclone Drive client

This is the home page of a private Google OAuth client. One person uses it to run the open-source rclone tool against their own Google Drive.

It is not a public app. It has no sign-up, no other users and no server. Its only job is to let rclone, running on its owner's own computer, upload files to and read documents from the owner's own Google Drive.
